# Roadmap

## Done: devotional theme and library (this release)

Deity paintings in temple arches with marigold garlands, a toran across the home page, a palm-leaf shelf for the library, lotus dividers and a petal shower. Six categories, deity pages, a chapter-by-chapter Gita reader, and 30 texts written in a plain-text format that a build script checks.

## Done: Navratri and Hindi meanings (October 2026)

A Navratri section with a page for each of the nine nights; the Durga Chalisa, Bhavani Ashtakam, Durge Durghat Bhari, Tulsidas’s Gauri stuti, the Navdurga meditation verses and two Saptashati mantras; the Durga Saptashati as a granth with chapters 4 and 11, the Ya Devi hymn of chapter 5, and summaries of the rest; a Hindi meaning for every verse, with a reader toggle; and a home button on every inner page.

The complete Durga Saptashati: all 13 chapters with their dhyana verses, checked verse by verse against one online edition with Gita Press numbering, plus the Devi Kavach, Argala Stotram and Keelak, and the Kali aarti Mangal Ki Seva.

## Next: content

1. **Proofread what exists.** Check every line against a named printed edition, record it in a `source:` line, and show a “proofread” mark on the text.
2. **The most requested texts:** Shiv, Ganesh, Lakshmi and Saraswati Chalisas; Bajrang Baan; Shiv Tandav Stotram; Mahishasura Mardini Stotram; Aditya Hridayam for Surya and Chhath; Vishnu Sahasranama; the Sundarkand; the rest of the Gita.
3. **Review the Hindi meanings** with a native Hindi reader, starting with the Awadhi and Braj texts.
4. **Regional collections**, each in its own script: the Marathi aarti set (Durge Durghat Bhari, Lavthavati Vikrala, Ghalin Lotangan), Gujarati, Bengali, Odia, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada traditions.
5. **Sources and rights.** Only public-domain texts: traditional works, saint-poets, and printings old enough to be free. Film songs and modern bhajans are copyrighted and stay out. Translations are written for the project, or taken from translations old enough to be in the public domain. Check each digital source’s terms (GRETIL, Wikisource, sanskritdocuments.org) before importing from it.

## Then: reach and speed

- A static page per text, so search engines index them and WhatsApp shows a proper preview when a link is shared
- Offline use after the first visit, for temples with weak signal
- Copies of the paintings in the repository instead of loading them from Wikimedia
- Load long granths a chapter at a time once the library grows
- A Hindi interface option
- Audio, recorded for the project

## Ongoing: corrections from readers

Every text links to a pre-filled GitHub issue for corrections. A correction is accepted once it is checked against a printed edition.
