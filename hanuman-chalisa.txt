id: hanuman-chalisa
kind: chalisa
deity: hanuman
lang: hi
language: Hindi
dialect: Awadhi
region: All of North India, and wherever Hindi speakers live
title: हनुमान चालीसा | Hanuman Chalisa | Forty verses in praise of Shri Hanuman
author: Goswami Tulsidas, 16th century. The fortieth chaupai carries his name. | attributed
occasions: hanuman-jayanti, sundarkand, daily
when: Recited on Tuesdays and Saturdays, morning or night, and in any time of fear or difficulty. On special days many recite it seven, eleven or a hundred and eight times.
notes: Editions differ in small ways, for example “संकर सुवन केसरीनंदन” or “शंकर स्वयं केसरीनंदन” in the sixth chaupai. The two dohas at the start and the one at the end are part of the text but not counted among the forty.

--- दोहा
श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज, निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि।
बरनउँ रघुबर बिमल जसु, जो दायकु फल चारि॥
~ Shriguru charan saroj raj, nij manu mukuru sudhari,
~ Baranaun Raghubar bimal jasu, jo dayaku phal chari.
= Cleaning the mirror of my mind with the dust of my Guru’s lotus feet, I sing the spotless glory of Shri Ram, which grants the four aims of life.

--- दोहा
बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके, सुमिरौं पवन-कुमार।
बल बुधि बिद्या देहु मोहिं, हरहु कलेस बिकार॥
~ Buddhiheen tanu janike, sumiraun Pavan-kumar,
~ Bal buddhi bidya dehu mohin, harahu kalesh bikar.
= Knowing how little wisdom I have, I remember the son of the Wind. Give me strength, understanding and knowledge, and take away my troubles and faults.

---
जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर।
जय कपीस तिहुँ लोक उजागर॥
~ Jai Hanuman gyan gun sagar,
~ jai Kapis tihun lok ujagar.
= Victory to Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and virtue! Victory to the lord of the monkeys, whose light fills the three worlds.

---
राम दूत अतुलित बल धामा।
अंजनि-पुत्र पवनसुत नामा॥
~ Ram doot atulit bal dhama,
~ Anjani-putra Pavansut nama.
= Ram’s messenger, home of matchless strength, known as Anjani’s son and the son of the Wind.

---
महाबीर बिक्रम बजरंगी।
कुमति निवार सुमति के संगी॥
~ Mahabir bikram Bajrangi,
~ kumati nivar sumati ke sangi.
= Great hero, mighty one with a body like a thunderbolt, you drive away bad thoughts and stand beside good sense.

---
कंचन बरन बिराज सुबेसा।
कानन कुंडल कुंचित केसा॥
~ Kanchan baran biraj subesa,
~ kanan kundal kunchit kesa.
= Golden in colour and finely dressed, with rings in your ears and curling hair.

---
हाथ बज्र औ ध्वजा बिराजै।
काँधे मूँज जनेऊ साजै॥
~ Haath bajra au dhwaja birajai,
~ kaandhe moonj janeu saajai.
= The thunderbolt and the banner shine in your hands, and a sacred thread of munja grass crosses your shoulder.

---
संकर सुवन केसरीनंदन।
तेज प्रताप महा जग बंदन॥
~ Sankar suvan Kesarinandan,
~ tej pratap maha jag bandan.
= Born of Shankar, joy of Kesari, the whole world bows to your radiance and might.

---
बिद्यावान गुनी अति चातुर।
राम काज करिबे को आतुर॥
~ Bidyavan guni ati chatur,
~ Ram kaj karibe ko aatur.
= Learned, virtuous and wise, you are always eager to do Ram’s work.

---
प्रभु चरित्र सुनिबे को रसिया।
राम लखन सीता मन बसिया॥
~ Prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiya,
~ Ram Lakhan Sita man basiya.
= You delight in hearing the Lord’s story, and Ram, Lakshman and Sita live in your heart.

---
सूक्ष्म रूप धरि सियहिं दिखावा।
बिकट रूप धरि लंक जरावा॥
~ Sukshma roop dhari Siyahin dikhava,
~ bikat roop dhari Lank jarava.
= Taking a tiny form you appeared before Sita; taking a terrible form you set Lanka on fire.

---
भीम रूप धरि असुर सँहारे।
रामचंद्र के काज सँवारे॥
~ Bheem roop dhari asur sanhare,
~ Ramchandra ke kaj sanvare.
= Taking a fearsome form you destroyed the demons and completed Ramchandra’s work.

---
लाय सजीवन लखन जियाये।
श्रीरघुबीर हरषि उर लाये॥
~ Laay Sajivan Lakhan jiyaye,
~ Shri Raghubir harashi ur laye.
= You brought the Sanjivani herb and revived Lakshman, and Shri Ram held you to his heart in joy.

---
रघुपति कीन्ही बहुत बड़ाई।
तुम मम प्रिय भरतहि सम भाई॥
~ Raghupati keenhi bahut badai,
~ tum mam priya Bharatahi sam bhai.
= Raghupati praised you greatly: “You are as dear to me as my brother Bharat.”

---
सहस बदन तुम्हरो जस गावैं।
अस कहि श्रीपति कंठ लगावैं॥
~ Sahas badan tumharo jas gavain,
~ as kahi Shripati kanth lagavain.
= “May the thousand-mouthed Shesh sing your praise,” said the Lord, and embraced you.

---
सनकादिक ब्रह्मादि मुनीसा।
नारद सारद सहित अहीसा॥
~ Sanakadik Brahmadi munisa,
~ Narad Sarad sahit Ahisa.
= Sanaka and the sages, Brahma and the gods, Narad, Saraswati and the king of serpents,

---
जम कुबेर दिगपाल जहाँ ते।
कबि कोबिद कहि सके कहाँ ते॥
~ Jam Kuber Digpal jahan te,
~ kabi kobid kahi sake kahan te.
= Yama, Kuber and the guardians of the directions cannot describe your glory, so how could poets and scholars?

---
तुम उपकार सुग्रीवहिं कीन्हा।
राम मिलाय राज पद दीन्हा॥
~ Tum upkar Sugrivahin keenha,
~ Ram milay raj pad deenha.
= You did Sugriv a great service: you brought him to Ram, and he regained his throne.

---
तुम्हरो मंत्र बिभीषन माना।
लंकेस्वर भए सब जग जाना॥
~ Tumharo mantra Bibhishan mana,
~ Lankeshwar bhaye sab jag jana.
= Vibhishan took your advice and became king of Lanka, as the whole world knows.

---
जुग सहस्र जोजन पर भानू।
लील्यो ताहि मधुर फल जानू॥
~ Jug sahasra jojan par bhanu,
~ leelyo tahi madhur phal janu.
= The sun, thousands of leagues away, you swallowed, taking it for a sweet fruit.

---
प्रभु मुद्रिका मेलि मुख माहीं।
जलधि लाँघि गये अचरज नाहीं॥
~ Prabhu mudrika meli mukh mahin,
~ jaladhi laanghi gaye achraj nahin.
= With the Lord’s ring in your mouth you leapt across the ocean. No wonder in that!

---
दुर्गम काज जगत के जेते।
सुगम अनुग्रह तुम्हरे तेते॥
~ Durgam kaj jagat ke jete,
~ sugam anugrah tumhre tete.
= Every hard task in the world becomes easy by your grace.

---
राम दुआरे तुम रखवारे।
होत न आज्ञा बिनु पैसारे॥
~ Ram duare tum rakhvare,
~ hot na agya binu paisare.
= You guard Ram’s door; no one enters without your leave.

---
सब सुख लहै तुम्हारी सरना।
तुम रच्छक काहू को डर ना॥
~ Sab sukh lahai tumhari sarna,
~ tum rakshak kahu ko dar na.
= Those who take refuge in you find every happiness. With you as protector, there is nothing to fear.

---
आपन तेज सम्हारो आपै।
तीनों लोक हाँक तें काँपै॥
~ Aapan tej samharo aapai,
~ teenon lok haank te kaanpai.
= Only you can hold your own power; the three worlds tremble at your roar.

---
भूत पिसाच निकट नहिं आवै।
महाबीर जब नाम सुनावै॥
~ Bhoot pisach nikat nahin aavai,
~ Mahabir jab naam sunavai.
= Ghosts and evil spirits stay away when the great hero’s name is spoken.

---
नासै रोग हरै सब पीरा।
जपत निरंतर हनुमत बीरा॥
~ Nasai rog harai sab peera,
~ japat nirantar Hanumat beera.
= Illness ends and every pain is removed by constantly repeating the name of brave Hanuman.

---
संकट तें हनुमान छुड़ावै।
मन क्रम बचन ध्यान जो लावै॥
~ Sankat te Hanuman chhudavai,
~ man kram bachan dhyan jo lavai.
= Hanuman frees from trouble all who remember him in thought, deed and word.

---
सब पर राम तपस्वी राजा।
तिन के काज सकल तुम साजा॥
~ Sab par Ram tapasvi raja,
~ tin ke kaj sakal tum saja.
= Ram, the ascetic king, is above all, and you carried out all his work.

---
और मनोरथ जो कोई लावै।
सोइ अमित जीवन फल पावै॥
~ Aur manorath jo koi lavai,
~ soi amit jeevan phal pavai.
= Whoever comes to you with any wish receives the boundless fruit of life.

---
चारों जुग परताप तुम्हारा।
है परसिद्ध जगत उजियारा॥
~ Charon jug partap tumhara,
~ hai parsiddh jagat ujiyara.
= Your glory shines through all four ages and lights up the world.

---
साधु संत के तुम रखवारे।
असुर निकंदन राम दुलारे॥
~ Sadhu sant ke tum rakhvare,
~ asur nikandan Ram dulare.
= You protect the good and the saintly, destroy demons, and are dear to Ram.

---
अष्ट सिद्धि नौ निधि के दाता।
अस बर दीन जानकी माता॥
~ Ashta siddhi nau nidhi ke data,
~ as bar deen Janaki mata.
= Mother Janaki blessed you to be the giver of the eight powers and the nine treasures.

---
राम रसायन तुम्हरे पासा।
सदा रहो रघुपति के दासा॥
~ Ram rasayan tumhre pasa,
~ sada raho Raghupati ke dasa.
= You hold the healing nectar of Ram’s name, and remain forever Raghupati’s servant.

---
तुम्हरे भजन राम को पावै।
जनम जनम के दुख बिसरावै॥
~ Tumhre bhajan Ram ko pavai,
~ janam janam ke dukh bisravai.
= Through devotion to you one reaches Ram and forgets the sorrows of many lives.

---
अंत काल रघुबर पुर जाई।
जहाँ जन्म हरि-भक्त कहाई॥
~ Ant kaal Raghubar pur jai,
~ jahan janma Hari-bhakt kahai.
= At life’s end one goes to Ram’s abode, and wherever born again is known as a devotee of Hari.

---
और देवता चित्त न धरई।
हनुमत सेइ सर्ब सुख करई॥
~ Aur devta chitt na dharai,
~ Hanumat sei sarb sukh karai.
= Even without turning the mind to other gods, serving Hanuman brings every happiness.

---
संकट कटै मिटै सब पीरा।
जो सुमिरै हनुमत बलबीरा॥
~ Sankat katai mitai sab peera,
~ jo sumirai Hanumat balbeera.
= Troubles are cut away and all pain ends for one who remembers mighty Hanuman.

---
जै जै जै हनुमान गोसाईं।
कृपा करहु गुरुदेव की नाईं॥
~ Jai jai jai Hanuman Gosain,
~ kripa karahu Gurudev ki nain.
= Victory, victory, victory to Lord Hanuman! Be gracious to me as a guru is to his disciple.

---
जो सत बार पाठ कर कोई।
छूटहि बंदि महा सुख होई॥
~ Jo sat baar paath kar koi,
~ chhootahi bandi maha sukh hoi.
= Whoever recites this a hundred times is freed from bondage and finds great joy.

---
जो यह पढ़ै हनुमान चालीसा।
होय सिद्धि साखी गौरीसा॥
~ Jo yah padhai Hanuman Chalisa,
~ hoy siddhi sakhi Gaurisa.
= Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa succeeds; Shiv, lord of Gauri, is witness to it.

---
तुलसीदास सदा हरि चेरा।
कीजै नाथ हृदय महँ डेरा॥
~ Tulsidas sada Hari chera,
~ keejai nath hriday mahan dera.
= Tulsidas is forever Hari’s servant. Lord, make your home in my heart.

--- दोहा
पवनतनय संकट हरन, मंगल मूरति रूप।
राम लखन सीता सहित, हृदय बसहु सुर भूप॥
~ Pavantanay sankat haran, mangal murati roop,
~ Ram Lakhan Sita sahit, hriday basahu sur bhoop.
= Son of the Wind, remover of troubles, form of all that is auspicious: king of the gods, live in my heart together with Ram, Lakshman and Sita.
