या कुन्देन्दुतुषारहारधवला या शुभ्रवस्त्रावृता।
या वीणावरदण्डमण्डितकरा या श्वेतपद्मासना॥
या ब्रह्माच्युतशंकरप्रभृतिभिर्देवैः सदा वन्दिता।
सा मां पातु सरस्वती भगवती निःशेषजाड्यापहा॥
~ Ya kundendu tushara hara dhavala, ya shubhra vastravrita,
~ Ya veena varadanda manditakara, ya shveta padmasana,
~ Ya Brahmachyuta Shankara prabhritibhir devaih sada vandita,
~ Sa mam patu Saraswati Bhagavati nihshesha jadyapaha.
= She who is white as jasmine, the moon, snow and a string of pearls, dressed in white; whose hands are graced by the veena and the staff of blessing; who sits on a white lotus; who is always worshipped by Brahma, Vishnu, Shiv and all the gods: may that Goddess Saraswati, who removes all dullness of mind, protect me.
