त्वमेव माता च पिता त्वमेव। त्वमेव बन्धुश्च सखा त्वमेव॥
त्वमेव विद्या द्रविणं त्वमेव। त्वमेव सर्वं मम देव देव॥
~ Tvameva mata cha pita tvameva, tvameva bandhushcha sakha tvameva,
~ Tvameva vidya dravinam tvameva, tvameva sarvam mama deva deva.
= You alone are my mother and my father, you alone my family and my friend. You alone are my knowledge and my wealth. You are everything to me, God of gods.
