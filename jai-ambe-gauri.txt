id: jai-ambe-gauri
kind: aarti
deity: durga
lang: hi
language: Hindi
region: North and West India
title: जय अम्बे गौरी | Jai Ambe Gauri | Aarti of Maa Durga
author: Traditional. The closing verse names Swami Shivanand. | attributed
occasions: navratri, dussehra, daily
when: The central aarti of Navratri across North India, sung morning and evening through the nine nights, and on Tuesdays and Fridays.
notes: Wording varies between editions: “हरि ब्रह्मा शिवरी” or “शिवजी” in the refrain, and the four-arms line appears with either “वरमुद्रा धारी” or “खड्ग खप्पर धारी”.
refrain-first: yes

--- refrain
जय अम्बे गौरी, मैया जय श्यामा गौरी।
तुमको निशदिन ध्यावत, हरि ब्रह्मा शिवरी॥
~ Jai Ambe Gauri, Maiya jai Shyama Gauri,
~ Tumko nishdin dhyavat, Hari Brahma Shivri.
= Glory to Mother Ambe, to Gauri, to Shyama! Vishnu, Brahma and Shiv meditate on you day and night.

---
माँग सिन्दूर विराजत, टीको मृगमद को।
उज्ज्वल से दोउ नैना, चन्द्रवदन नीको॥
~ Maang sindoor virajat, teeko mrigmad ko,
~ Ujjwal se dou naina, chandravadan neeko.
= Vermilion graces the parting of your hair, with a tilak of musk; your eyes are bright and your face is lovely as the moon.

---
कनक समान कलेवर, रक्ताम्बर राजै।
रक्तपुष्प गल माला, कण्ठन पर साजै॥
~ Kanak samaan kalevar, raktambar raajai,
~ Raktapushp gal mala, kanthan par saajai.
= Your form shines like gold, dressed in red, and a garland of red flowers adorns your neck.

---
केहरि वाहन राजत, खड्ग खप्पर धारी।
सुर नर मुनि जन सेवत, तिनके दुखहारी॥
~ Kehari vahan raajat, khadg khappar dhari,
~ Sur nar muni jan sevat, tinke dukhhari.
= You ride the lion, holding sword and skull-bowl. Gods, humans and sages serve you, and you take away their sorrows.

---
कानन कुण्डल शोभित, नासाग्रे मोती।
कोटिक चन्द्र दिवाकर, सम राजत ज्योती॥
~ Kaanan kundal shobhit, nasagre moti,
~ Kotik chandra divakar, sam raajat jyoti.
= Earrings shine in your ears and a pearl at your nose; your radiance equals millions of moons and suns.

---
शुम्भ निशुम्भ बिदारे, महिषासुर घाती।
धूम्र विलोचन नैना, निशदिन मदमाती॥
~ Shumbh Nishumbh bidaare, Mahishasur ghaati,
~ Dhoomra vilochan naina, nishdin madmaati.
= You tore apart Shumbh and Nishumbh and slew Mahishasur; you struck down Dhumralochan, your eyes ablaze with divine fervour day and night.

---
चण्ड मुण्ड संहारे, शोणित बीज हरे।
मधु कैटभ दोउ मारे, सुर भयहीन करे॥
~ Chand Mund sanhaare, shonit beej hare,
~ Madhu Kaitabh dou maare, sur bhayheen kare.
= You destroyed Chand and Mund and vanquished Raktabeej; you slew Madhu and Kaitabh and freed the gods from fear.

---
ब्रह्माणी रुद्राणी, तुम कमला रानी।
आगम निगम बखानी, तुम शिव पटरानी॥
~ Brahmani Rudrani, tum Kamla Rani,
~ Aagam nigam bakhani, tum Shiv patrani.
= You are Brahmani and Rudrani, and Queen Kamala; the scriptures sing your praise, chief queen of Shiv.

---
चौंसठ योगिनी मंगल गावत, नृत्य करत भैरों।
बाजत ताल मृदंगा, अरु बाजत डमरू॥
~ Chaunsath yogini mangal gaavat, nritya karat Bhairon,
~ Baajat taal mridanga, aru baajat damru.
= The sixty-four yoginis sing auspicious songs and Bhairav dances; cymbals and drums resound, and the damru too.

---
तुम ही जग की माता, तुम ही हो भरता।
भक्तन की दुख हरता, सुख सम्पति करता॥
~ Tum hi jag ki mata, tum hi ho bharta,
~ Bhaktan ki dukh harta, sukh sampati karta.
= You alone are the mother of the world and its sustainer; you remove your devotees’ sorrows and bring them happiness and plenty.

---
भुजा चार अति शोभित, वरमुद्रा धारी।
मनवांछित फल पावत, सेवत नर नारी॥
~ Bhuja chaar ati shobhit, varmudra dhari,
~ Manvanchhit phal paavat, sevat nar nari.
= Your four arms are radiant, one raised in blessing; the men and women who serve you receive what their hearts desire.

---
कंचन थाल विराजत, अगर कपूर बाती।
श्रीमालकेतु में राजत, कोटि रतन ज्योती॥
~ Kanchan thaal virajat, agar kapoor baati,
~ Shrimalketu mein raajat, koti ratan jyoti.
= A golden plate is set with aloe-wood, camphor and wicks, and the flames shine like countless jewels.

---
श्री अम्बेजी की आरती, जो कोई नर गावे।
कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी, सुख सम्पत्ति पावे॥
~ Shri Ambeji ki aarti, jo koi nar gaave,
~ Kahat Shivanand Swami, sukh sampatti paave.
= Whoever sings this aarti of Mother Ambe, says Swami Shivanand, gains happiness and prosperity.
