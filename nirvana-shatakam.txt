id: nirvana-shatakam
kind: stotra
deity: shiv
lang: sa
language: Sanskrit
region: All of India
title: निर्वाणषट्कम् | Nirvana Shatakam (Shivoham) | Six verses on the true Self
author: Traditionally attributed to Adi Shankaracharya | attributed
occasions: mahashivratri, daily
when: A hymn of Advaita Vedanta, chanted in meditation and on Maha Shivratri. Here “Shiv” means the pure Self, beyond body and mind.
notes: Also called Atma Shatakam. The last verse has variants, such as “न चासङ्गतं नैव मुक्तिर्न मेयः”.

---
मनोबुद्ध्यहङ्कारचित्तानि नाहं न च श्रोत्रजिह्वे न च घ्राणनेत्रे।
न च व्योम भूमिर्न तेजो न वायुः चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम्॥
~ Mano buddhyahankara chittani naham, na cha shrotra jihve na cha ghrana netre,
~ Na cha vyoma bhumir na tejo na vayuh, chidananda roopah Shivo’ham Shivo’ham.
= I am not the mind, intellect, ego or memory; not the ears, tongue, nose or eyes; not space, earth, fire or air. I am consciousness and bliss. I am Shiv, I am Shiv.

---
न च प्राणसंज्ञो न वै पञ्चवायुः न वा सप्तधातुर्न वा पञ्चकोशः।
न वाक्पाणिपादं न चोपस्थपायू चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम्॥
~ Na cha prana sanjno na vai panchavayuh, na va saptadhatur na va panchakoshah,
~ Na vak pani padam na chopasthapayu, chidananda roopah Shivo’ham Shivo’ham.
= I am not the breath of life nor the five vital airs, not the seven elements of the body nor its five sheaths, not speech, hands, feet or any organ of action. I am consciousness and bliss. I am Shiv, I am Shiv.

---
न मे द्वेषरागौ न मे लोभमोहौ मदो नैव मे नैव मात्सर्यभावः।
न धर्मो न चार्थो न कामो न मोक्षः चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम्॥
~ Na me dvesharagau na me lobhamohau, mado naiva me naiva matsarya bhavah,
~ Na dharmo na chartho na kamo na mokshah, chidananda roopah Shivo’ham Shivo’ham.
= I have no hatred or attachment, no greed or delusion, no pride and no envy. I am not duty, wealth, desire, or even liberation. I am consciousness and bliss. I am Shiv, I am Shiv.

---
न पुण्यं न पापं न सौख्यं न दुःखं न मन्त्रो न तीर्थं न वेदा न यज्ञाः।
अहं भोजनं नैव भोज्यं न भोक्ता चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम्॥
~ Na punyam na papam na saukhyam na duhkham, na mantro na tirtham na veda na yajnah,
~ Aham bhojanam naiva bhojyam na bhokta, chidananda roopah Shivo’ham Shivo’ham.
= Not merit or sin, not pleasure or pain; not mantra, pilgrimage, scripture or sacrifice. I am not the eating, the food, or the one who eats. I am consciousness and bliss. I am Shiv, I am Shiv.

---
न मे मृत्युशङ्का न मे जातिभेदः पिता नैव मे नैव माता न जन्म।
न बन्धुर्न मित्रं गुरुर्नैव शिष्यः चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम्॥
~ Na me mrityushanka na me jatibhedah, pita naiva me naiva mata na janma,
~ Na bandhur na mitram gurur naiva shishyah, chidananda roopah Shivo’ham Shivo’ham.
= I have no fear of death and no divisions of birth; no father, no mother, no birth at all; no kin, no friend, no teacher and no disciple. I am consciousness and bliss. I am Shiv, I am Shiv.

---
अहं निर्विकल्पो निराकाररूपो विभुत्वाच्च सर्वत्र सर्वेन्द्रियाणाम्।
सदा मे समत्वं न मुक्तिर्न बन्धः चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहं शिवोऽहम्॥
~ Aham nirvikalpo nirakara roopo, vibhutvachcha sarvatra sarvendriyanam,
~ Sada me samatvam na muktir na bandhah, chidananda roopah Shivo’ham Shivo’ham.
= I am free of all doubt and without form, present everywhere and behind every sense. I am always the same; for me there is no freedom and no bondage. I am consciousness and bliss. I am Shiv, I am Shiv.
