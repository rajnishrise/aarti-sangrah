author: Rishi Vishwamitra, Rigveda 3.62.10 | attributed

ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः।
तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि।
धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्॥
~ Om bhur bhuvah svah,
~ Tat savitur varenyam bhargo devasya dhimahi,
~ Dhiyo yo nah prachodayat.
= Om. Earth, sky and heaven. We meditate on the glorious light of the divine Savitr, the Sun, worthy of all worship. May he inspire and guide our minds.
