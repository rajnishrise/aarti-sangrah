title: महामृत्युंजय मंत्र | Mahamrityunjaya Mantra | The great prayer for victory over death
author: Rishi Vasishtha, Rigveda 7.59.12 | attributed
occasions: shravan, mahashivratri, daily
when: Chanted for healing, protection and peace, especially when someone is unwell, often 108 times on a rudraksha mala.

---
ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम्।
उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान्मृत्योर्मुक्षीय माऽमृतात्॥
~ Om tryambakam yajamahe sugandhim pushtivardhanam,
~ Urvarukamiva bandhanan mrityor mukshiya ma’mritat.
= We worship the three-eyed one, fragrant, who nourishes all beings. As a ripe cucumber is freed from its vine, may he free us from death, and never from immortality.
