id: ya-devi-sarvabhuteshu
kind: mantra
form: Shloka
deity: durga
lang: sa
language: Sanskrit
region: All of India
title: या देवी सर्वभूतेषु | Ya Devi Sarvabhuteshu | To the Goddess present in every being
author: From the Devi Mahatmya (Durga Saptashati), chapter 5 | known
occasions: navratri, daily
when: Chanted through Navratri, often as the opening of Devi puja. In the Saptashati the same verse is repeated for twenty-one forms of the Goddess; the five here are the ones most often chanted on their own. The full hymn is in the Durga Saptashati, chapter 5.
notes: Each verse is recited with the line “नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः” three times over, which is why printed editions give every verse three numbers.

---
या देवी सर्वभूतेषु शक्तिरूपेण संस्थिता।
नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः॥
~ Ya Devi sarvabhuteshu shaktirupena sansthita,
~ Namastasyai namastasyai namastasyai namo namah.
= To the Goddess who dwells in all beings as power: salutations to her, salutations to her, salutations to her, again and again.
+ जो देवी सब प्राणियों में शक्ति के रूप में स्थित हैं, उनको नमस्कार है, उनको नमस्कार है, उनको बार-बार नमस्कार है।

---
या देवी सर्वभूतेषु मातृरूपेण संस्थिता।
नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः॥
~ Ya Devi sarvabhuteshu matrirupena sansthita,
~ Namastasyai namastasyai namastasyai namo namah.
= To the Goddess who dwells in all beings as mother: salutations to her, again and again.
+ जो देवी सब प्राणियों में माता के रूप में स्थित हैं, उनको बार-बार नमस्कार है।

---
या देवी सर्वभूतेषु लक्ष्मीरूपेण संस्थिता।
नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः॥
~ Ya Devi sarvabhuteshu Lakshmirupena sansthita,
~ Namastasyai namastasyai namastasyai namo namah.
= To the Goddess who dwells in all beings as prosperity: salutations to her, again and again.
+ जो देवी सब प्राणियों में लक्ष्मी (समृद्धि) के रूप में स्थित हैं, उनको बार-बार नमस्कार है।

---
या देवी सर्वभूतेषु बुद्धिरूपेण संस्थिता।
नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः॥
~ Ya Devi sarvabhuteshu buddhirupena sansthita,
~ Namastasyai namastasyai namastasyai namo namah.
= To the Goddess who dwells in all beings as intelligence: salutations to her, again and again.
+ जो देवी सब प्राणियों में बुद्धि के रूप में स्थित हैं, उनको बार-बार नमस्कार है।

---
या देवी सर्वभूतेषु शान्तिरूपेण संस्थिता।
नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः॥
~ Ya Devi sarvabhuteshu shantirupena sansthita,
~ Namastasyai namastasyai namastasyai namo namah.
= To the Goddess who dwells in all beings as peace: salutations to her, again and again.
+ जो देवी सब प्राणियों में शान्ति के रूप में स्थित हैं, उनको बार-बार नमस्कार है।
