title: असतो मा सद्गमय | Asato Ma Sadgamaya | From darkness to light
author: Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.3.28 | known
occasions: morning, daily
when: Recited at the start of study, at school assemblies and at the opening of gatherings.

---
ॐ असतो मा सद्गमय।
तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय।
मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय।
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥
~ Om asato ma sadgamaya,
~ Tamaso ma jyotirgamaya,
~ Mrityorma amritam gamaya,
~ Om shantih shantih shantih.
= Lead me from the unreal to the real. Lead me from darkness to light. Lead me from death to immortality. Om, peace, peace, peace.
