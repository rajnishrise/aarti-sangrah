id: hare-krishna-mahamantra
kind: mantra
form: Mahamantra
deity: krishna
lang: sa
language: Sanskrit
region: All of India and worldwide
title: हरे राम हरे कृष्ण | Hare Rama Hare Krishna | The mahamantra of sixteen names
author: Kali-Santarana Upanishad | known
occasions: janmashtami, daily
when: Repeated in japa on a mala of 108 beads and sung in kirtan.
notes: The Kali-Santarana Upanishad gives it beginning with “Hare Rama”; ISKCON and many kirtan groups begin with “Hare Krishna”.

---
हरे राम हरे राम राम राम हरे हरे।
हरे कृष्ण हरे कृष्ण कृष्ण कृष्ण हरे हरे॥
~ Hare Rama Hare Rama, Rama Rama Hare Hare,
~ Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna Hare Hare.
= A call to the Lord through his names Hari, Ram and Krishna, repeated with love until the mind rests in them.
