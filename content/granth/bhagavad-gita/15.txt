chapter: 15
intro: Krishna describes the world as an upside-down tree to be cut down with detachment, explains how the soul moves from body to body, and reveals himself as Purushottam, the Supreme Person beyond both the perishable and the imperishable. Many ashrams and families recite this chapter before meals.

---
> श्रीभगवानुवाच | The Lord said
ऊर्ध्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम्।
छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि यस्तं वेद स वेदवित्॥
~ Urdhvamoolamadhahshakhamashvattham prahuravyayam,
~ Chhandamsi yasya parnani yastam veda sa vedavit.
= They speak of an eternal ashvattha tree with its roots above and its branches below, whose leaves are the Vedic hymns. Whoever knows this tree knows the Vedas.

---
अधश्चोर्ध्वं प्रसृतास्तस्य शाखा गुणप्रवृद्धा विषयप्रवालाः।
अधश्च मूलान्यनुसन्ततानि कर्मानुबन्धीनि मनुष्यलोके॥
~ Adhashchordhvam prasritastasya shakha gunapravriddha vishayapravalah,
~ Adhashcha moolanyanusantatani karmanubandhini manushyaloke.
= Its branches spread below and above, fed by the gunas, with the objects of the senses as their buds. Below, in the human world, its roots spread out, binding beings to action.

---
न रूपमस्येह तथोपलभ्यते नान्तो न चादिर्न च सम्प्रतिष्ठा।
अश्वत्थमेनं सुविरूढमूलमसङ्गशस्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा॥
~ Na roopamasyeha tathopalabhyate nanto na chadirna cha sampratishtha,
~ Ashvatthamenam suviroodhamoolamasangashastrena dridhena chhittva.
= Its real form cannot be seen here, nor its end, nor its beginning, nor what it stands on. Cutting down this deep-rooted tree with the strong axe of detachment,

---
ततः पदं तत्परिमार्गितव्यं यस्मिन्गता न निवर्तन्ति भूयः।
तमेव चाद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी॥
~ Tatah padam tatparimargitavyam yasmingata na nivartanti bhuyah,
~ Tameva chadyam purusham prapadye yatah pravrittih prasrita purani.
= one should seek that state from which there is no return, saying: “I take refuge in that first Person from whom this ancient stream of creation flows.”

---
निर्मानमोहा जितसङ्गदोषा अध्यात्मनित्या विनिवृत्तकामाः।
द्वन्द्वैर्विमुक्ताः सुखदुःखसंज्ञैर्गच्छन्त्यमूढाः पदमव्ययं तत्॥
~ Nirmanamoha jitasangadosha adhyatmanitya vinivrittakamah,
~ Dvandvairvimuktah sukhaduhkhasanjnairgachchhantyamoodhah padamavyayam tat.
= Free from pride and delusion, having conquered attachment, always dwelling on the Self, with desires stilled and released from the opposites called pleasure and pain, the wise reach that eternal state.

---
न तद्भासयते सूर्यो न शशाङ्को न पावकः।
यद्गत्वा न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम॥
~ Na tadbhasayate suryo na shashanko na pavakah,
~ Yadgatva na nivartante taddhama paramam mama.
= No sun, moon or fire lights that place. Those who reach it never return: that is my supreme home.

---
ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः।
मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति॥
~ Mamaivamsho jivaloke jivabhootah sanatanah,
~ Manahshashthanindriyani prakritisthani karshati.
= An eternal part of me becomes the living soul in the world of beings, and draws to itself the five senses and the mind, which belong to nature.

---
शरीरं यदवाप्नोति यच्चाप्युत्क्रामतीश्वरः।
गृहीत्वैतानि संयाति वायुर्गन्धानिवाशयात्॥
~ Shariram yadavapnoti yachchapyutkramatishvarah,
~ Grihitvaitani samyati vayurgandhanivashayat.
= When the soul, the master of the body, takes up a body or leaves it, it carries these along, as the wind carries scents from flowers.

---
श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च रसनं घ्राणमेव च।
अधिष्ठाय मनश्चायं विषयानुपसेवते॥
~ Shrotram chakshuh sparshanam cha rasanam ghranameva cha,
~ Adhishthaya manashchayam vishayanupasevate.
= Presiding over hearing, sight, touch, taste and smell, and over the mind, it experiences the objects of the senses.

---
उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम्।
विमूढा नानुपश्यन्ति पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः॥
~ Utkramantam sthitam vapi bhunjanam va gunanvitam,
~ Vimoodha nanupashyanti pashyanti jnanachakshushah.
= The deluded do not see it leaving the body, staying in it, or enjoying the world through the gunas. Those with the eye of wisdom see it.

---
यतन्तो योगिनश्चैनं पश्यन्त्यात्मन्यवस्थितम्।
यतन्तोऽप्यकृतात्मानो नैनं पश्यन्त्यचेतसः॥
~ Yatanto yoginashchainam pashyantyatmanyavasthitam,
~ Yatanto’pyakritatmano nainam pashyantyachetasah.
= Yogis who strive see it dwelling within themselves; but the unprepared and unaware do not see it, even when they try.

---
यदादित्यगतं तेजो जगद्भासयतेऽखिलम्।
यच्चन्द्रमसि यच्चाग्नौ तत्तेजो विद्धि मामकम्॥
~ Yadadityagatam tejo jagadbhasayate’khilam,
~ Yachchandramasi yachchagnau tattejo viddhi mamakam.
= The light of the sun that brightens the whole world, and the light in the moon and in fire: know that light to be mine.

---
गामाविश्य च भूतानि धारयाम्यहमोजसा।
पुष्णामि चौषधीः सर्वाः सोमो भूत्वा रसात्मकः॥
~ Gamavishya cha bhootani dharayamyahamojasa,
~ Pushnami chaushadhih sarvah somo bhootva rasatmakah.
= Entering the earth, I hold up all beings with my strength; becoming the moon, full of sap, I nourish every plant.

---
अहं वैश्वानरो भूत्वा प्राणिनां देहमाश्रितः।
प्राणापानसमायुक्तः पचाम्यन्नं चतुर्विधम्॥
~ Aham vaishvanaro bhootva praninam dehamashritah,
~ Pranapanasamayuktah pachamyannam chaturvidham.
= Becoming the digestive fire in the bodies of living beings, joined with the in-breath and out-breath, I digest the four kinds of food.

---
सर्वस्य चाहं हृदि सन्निविष्टो मत्तः स्मृतिर्ज्ञानमपोहनं च।
वेदैश्च सर्वैरहमेव वेद्यो वेदान्तकृद्वेदविदेव चाहम्॥
~ Sarvasya chaham hridi sannivishto mattah smritirjnanamapohanam cha,
~ Vedaishcha sarvairahameva vedyo vedantakridvedavideva chaham.
= I am seated in the hearts of all; from me come memory, knowledge and their loss. I alone am what all the Vedas seek to know; I am the author of the Vedanta and the knower of the Vedas.

---
द्वाविमौ पुरुषौ लोके क्षरश्चाक्षर एव च।
क्षरः सर्वाणि भूतानि कूटस्थोऽक्षर उच्यते॥
~ Dvavimau purushau loke ksharashchakshara eva cha,
~ Ksharah sarvani bhootani kootastho’kshara uchyate.
= There are two persons in this world, the perishable and the imperishable. All beings are perishable; the unchanging one is called imperishable.

---
उत्तमः पुरुषस्त्वन्यः परमात्मेत्युदाहृतः।
यो लोकत्रयमाविश्य बिभर्त्यव्यय ईश्वरः॥
~ Uttamah purushastvanyah paramatmetyudahritah,
~ Yo lokatrayamavishya bibhartyavyaya ishvarah.
= But there is another, the highest Person, called the Supreme Self: the eternal Lord who enters the three worlds and sustains them.

---
यस्मात्क्षरमतीतोऽहमक्षरादपि चोत्तमः।
अतोऽस्मि लोके वेदे च प्रथितः पुरुषोत्तमः॥
~ Yasmatksharamatito’hamaksharadapi chottamah,
~ Ato’smi loke vede cha prathitah purushottamah.
= Because I am beyond the perishable and higher even than the imperishable, I am known in the world and in the Veda as Purushottam, the Supreme Person.

---
यो मामेवमसम्मूढो जानाति पुरुषोत्तमम्।
स सर्वविद्भजति मां सर्वभावेन भारत॥
~ Yo mamevamasammoodho janati purushottamam,
~ Sa sarvavidbhajati mam sarvabhavena Bharata.
= Whoever, free from delusion, knows me as the Supreme Person knows everything, and worships me with their whole being, Bharata.

---
इति गुह्यतमं शास्त्रमिदमुक्तं मयानघ।
एतद्बुद्ध्वा बुद्धिमान्स्यात्कृतकृत्यश्च भारत॥
~ Iti guhyatamam shastramidamuktam mayanagha,
~ Etadbuddhva buddhimansyatkritakrityashcha Bharata.
= So I have given you this most secret teaching, sinless one. Whoever understands it becomes truly wise and has done all there is to do, Bharata.

--- इति
ॐ तत्सदिति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे पुरुषोत्तमयोगो नाम पञ्चदशोऽध्यायः॥
~ Om tat sat. Iti Shrimad Bhagavad Gitasu Upanishatsu Brahmavidyayam Yogashastre Shri Krishnarjuna samvade Purushottamayogo nama panchadasho’dhyayah.
= Om, that is the truth. So ends the fifteenth chapter, the Yoga of the Supreme Person, in the dialogue between Shri Krishna and Arjun, the Upanishad of the Gita, the knowledge of Brahman and the scripture of yoga.
