kind: bhajan

deity: ram

language: Hindi
region: All of India
title: रघुपति राघव राजा राम | Raghupati Raghav Raja Ram | The Ram Dhun
author: Traditional Ram Dhun. The version with “ईश्वर अल्लाह तेरो नाम” was sung at Mahatma Gandhi’s prayer meetings. | traditional
occasions: ram-navami, daily
when: Sung in kirtan, at prayer meetings and school assemblies, and on 2 October.
notes: Gandhi’s version was sung on the Salt March of 1930. Older devotional forms repeat only the names of Sita and Ram.
refrain-first: yes

--- refrain
रघुपति राघव राजाराम, पतित पावन सीताराम॥
~ Raghupati Raghav Raja Ram, patit pavan Sitaram.
= Lord of the Raghus, King Ram, with Sita: purifier of the fallen.

---
सीताराम सीताराम, भज प्यारे तू सीताराम॥
~ Sitaram Sitaram, bhaj pyare tu Sitaram.
= Sita-Ram, Sita-Ram: dear one, sing the names of Sita and Ram.

---
ईश्वर अल्लाह तेरो नाम, सब को सन्मति दे भगवान॥
~ Ishwar Allah tero naam, sab ko sanmati de Bhagwan.
= Ishwar and Allah are both your names. Lord, give everyone wisdom and goodwill.
