--- refrain
आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की।
दुष्ट दलन रघुनाथ कला की॥
~ Aarti keejai Hanuman Lala ki,
~ Dusht dalan Raghunath kala ki.
= Offer the aarti to beloved Hanuman, crusher of the wicked, a living ray of Raghunath’s power.

---
जाके बल से गिरिवर काँपे।
रोग दोष जाके निकट न झाँके॥
~ Jake bal se girivar kaanpe,
~ Rog dosh jake nikat na jhaanke.
= At his strength even great mountains tremble; disease and misfortune dare not come near him.

---
अंजनि पुत्र महा बलदायी।
सन्तन के प्रभु सदा सहाई॥
~ Anjani putra maha baldayi,
~ Santan ke prabhu sada sahai.
= Son of Anjani, giver of great strength, the lord who always helps the good.

---
दे बीरा रघुनाथ पठाए।
लंका जारि सिया सुधि लाए॥
~ De beera Raghunath pathaye,
~ Lanka jaari Siya sudhi laye.
= Raghunath handed him the betel-leaf token and sent him forth; he burned Lanka and brought back news of Sita.

---
लंका सो कोट समुद्र सी खाई।
जात पवनसुत बार न लाई॥
~ Lanka so kot samudra si khai,
~ Jaat Pavansut baar na lai.
= Lanka was a fortress with the ocean for a moat, yet the son of the Wind crossed it without a moment’s delay.

---
लंका जारि असुर संहारे।
सियारामजी के काज सँवारे॥
~ Lanka jaari asur sanhaare,
~ Siyaramji ke kaaj sanvaare.
= He set Lanka ablaze, destroyed the demons and accomplished the work of Sita and Ram.

---
लक्ष्मण मूर्छित पड़े सकारे।
आनि संजीवन प्राण उबारे॥
~ Lakshman moorchhit pade sakare,
~ Aani sanjivan praan ubaare.
= When Lakshman lay unconscious, he brought the life-giving herb by dawn and saved his life.

---
पैठि पाताल तोरि जम कारे।
अहिरावण की भुजा उखारे॥
~ Paithi patal tori jam kaare,
~ Ahiravan ki bhuja ukhaare.
= He entered the netherworld, broke through its deadly defences and tore off Ahiravan’s arms.

---
बाएँ भुजा असुर दल मारे।
दाहिने भुजा सन्तजन तारे॥
~ Baayen bhuja asur dal maare,
~ Dahine bhuja santjan taare.
= With his left arm he struck down the demon armies; with his right he rescued the good.

---
सुर नर मुनि आरती उतारें।
जय जय जय हनुमान उचारें॥
~ Sur nar muni aarti utaaren,
~ Jai jai jai Hanuman uchaaren.
= Gods, humans and sages wave the aarti to him, calling out “Victory, victory, victory to Hanuman!”

---
कंचन थार कपूर लौ छाई।
आरती करत अंजना माई॥
~ Kanchan thaar kapoor lau chhai,
~ Aarti karat Anjana maai.
= With the camphor flame glowing on a golden plate, Mother Anjana herself performs his aarti.

---
जो हनुमानजी की आरती गावे।
बसि बैकुण्ठ परमपद पावे॥
~ Jo Hanumanji ki aarti gaave,
~ Basi Baikunth parampad paave.
= Whoever sings Hanuman’s aarti dwells in Vaikunth and attains the highest state.
